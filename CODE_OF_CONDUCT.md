# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

FrameScope contributors are expected to make participation respectful, constructive, and technically focused. Be considerate in reviews, critique ideas and code rather than people, and make room for contributors with different levels of experience.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, or sustained disruption of project spaces.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior violates this standard. Reports should be made privately to the maintainer through the contact channel listed by the eventual public repository when possible.

Maintainers should protect the privacy and safety of people who report conduct concerns and should apply consequences proportionally and consistently.

## Scope

This code applies in project spaces and when someone is representing FrameScope in public community spaces.
